Privacy Policy for DailyPoem

Developer: MintNest Studio
App: DailyPoem — Shayari & Poetry Reels (package com.dailypoem.app)
Last updated: 9 November 2026
Contact: mintnestsofficial@gmail.com

The short version

DailyPoem does not ask you to create an account, sign in, or give us your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal information. Your favourites, reading progress, and settings are stored only on your own device. We do not run a server that stores your personal data.

Information we do not collect

  • No account or login: there is no registration, no sign-in, and no per-user cloud profile.
  • No personal information: we do not ask for or store your name, email, phone number, address, or contacts.
  • No user content uploaded: photos you use in Photo Shayari stay on your phone — the app does not upload them anywhere.
  • No hand-typed Quranic text is collected or sent anywhere; the Noor section shows duas and hadiths with their sources.

Information stored on your device only

Favourites, novel reading progress, and app settings are saved in your device's local storage. They are not sent to us, and deleting the app deletes them.

Poetry, novels, scenes, and reels are bundled with the app or fetched from public content files when new material is published. Fetching content is a normal network request; the app itself sends no personal information with it.

Advertising (Unity Ads)

DailyPoem shows a small amount of advertising to keep the app free:

  • A banner only on the Poems (Library) tab.
  • An interstitial only after every third milestone (a novel episode completed or a photo exported), and never more than once in 4 minutes.
  • A rewarded ad only when you choose it yourself, from the “Support DailyPoem” row in Settings.

Advertising is served by Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). Unity's ad service may collect device and advertising identifiers as described in Unity's own privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy. We do not receive your name or contact details from Unity.

Sharing

Sharing happens only when you tap Share: the app hands the poem, image, or reel to your device's own share sheet, and you choose where it goes. We do not share anything on your behalf.

Children

DailyPoem contains poetry, novels, and religious (Noor) content and is not directed at collecting information from children. Since the app collects no personal information at all, there is nothing for a parent to ask us to delete — but you can reach us at the contact address above with any question.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new “Last updated” date. Because the app has no accounts, we cannot notify you individually; please check this page occasionally.
